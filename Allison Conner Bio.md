I was fully in charge of all aspects of front-of-the-house for the gallery, managing collector databases, press releases, and events. As a Publicity Associate at PolyGram Corporate, supporting Mercury Records' artists and media appearances.

Beyond design, I bring strong communications, project management, and operations skills. I've run retail and cash operations, drafted contracts and legal correspondence, and, through administrative roles with the State of Hawaiʻi, built experience in budgeting, procurement, grant compliance, and office administration.

My creative practice is grounded in a Bachelor of Arts in Art History from the University of Pennsylvania. I'm currently pursuing an MBA at the Shidler College of Business, University of Hawaiʻi at Mānoa.
