# Research Brief

## 1. Identify and explain the challenge: what it is, who it affects, and why it is a problem now rather than in general

The challenge is the ease, frequency, and effectiveness of cyberattacks on systems in the internet of things. Looking at the recent attacks on refrigerators connected to the internet.

This problem affects everybody. It increases costs for businesses and governments through a need to increase security and in response to the damage caused by these attacks. These costs are passed down to consumers through higher costs for items and services. The cyberattacks against the refrigerators worldwide has destroyed huge amounts of perishable foods.

AI has lowered the barrier for committing cyberattacks to machines connected to the internet. The return on investment on cyberattacks has now lowered to a point that they have become much more prevalent and need to be addressed.

The problem has a right now element because of the increased number of things connected to the internet. This combined with increased access to AI to do cyberattacks creates the environment for more attacks and more chaos from compromised appliances and machines.

## 2. Link it to the course's economics: micro (elasticity, market structure, incentives, who captures what), macro (growth, fiscal and monetary policy, trade), or both

This issue connects to both micro- and macro-economic issues.

Using AI has lowered the barrier to entry for cybercrimes. You no longer need to know about computer systems to cause damage or extort money from businesses. This means that the cost of doing these attacks have also gone down so attacks don’t need to make much money to have a useful ROI.

- **Micro:** The financial incentive for cybercrime has gone up as the cost of entering the market have dropped precipitously. It is elastic. As the cost of the attack has gone down and the number of attacks goes up.
- **Marco:** Increasing cyber attacks affects trade and growth.

## 3. Analyze implications: what the mechanism is doing now, and what it does next under conditions you name

The mechanism is the lower barrier to entry through AI use mixed with the increased number of devices connected to the internet making them vulnerable to attack. This increase in attacks and attackers mixed with a much larger attack surface is a recipe for disaster.

It causes chaos in every aspect of people worldwide.

This will be illustrated by comparing the rate of publicly disclosed attacks against reported outages against reported rates of Internet of Things devices. This will focus on the past five years, to cover the “natural experiment” of release of ChatGPT in 2023.

## 4. Propose policy or strategy, and defend it against the obvious objection

I propose that important systems must be taken off the internet.

Things like these refrigerators do not need to be connected to the internet at all. They could easily be air gapped. The monitoring systems can still be connected to the internet, although with more security, if they cannot interfere with the operation of the machine it is monitoring. Affecting change to the machines must be done in person and by a human so that this type of attack cannot happen.

I believe that the only way to keep machines safe at the current time is to keep those machines disconnected from the internet. It is time to look closely at what needs to be connected to the internet and widening the surface of attack. Of course there is resistance to disconnecting machines from the internet because it makes maintaining those machines cheaper and easier through remote access.
